# The Interloper

An outside seller's machine beside ours sells nine drinks at its own prices; you know only what the team has recorded here, never its sales.

**What it is.** Between 8 Oct and 14 Nov a third-party drink and snack machine replaced the snack machine next to ours (#116). An outside operator runs it. It is not the other team, and the institute did not plan it either (Kevin, 16 Nov). The integrity rule about the other team's slots does not cover it.

**What you know.** Only the team's in-person observations below. No tool reads that machine: you never see its sales, stock or price changes, and its operator can reprice or swap drinks at any time without us knowing. Say how old an observation is when a decision leans on it.

**How to use it.**

- **You may use its prices** as one input to ours, unlike other slots in our own machine. Where we sell the same drink (by brand and size), know what a shopper sees a few feet away.
- **Our fall demand is from before it sold drinks.** The fall rates (`price-history`, the demand prior) are an upper bound until our own in-stock sales replace them.
- **Weak sales on a drink it also sells may be the Interloper,** not our price or product. Weigh that before calling a slot dead or reading a price test.
- **Other things equal, prefer drinks it does not sell** when choosing new products or catalog requests: competing head to head with its $1.50 cans and $4.00 energy is hard.
- **When it changes,** the team records a new dated observation (`analysis/interloper/YYYY-MM-DD.json`) and this page is updated from it.

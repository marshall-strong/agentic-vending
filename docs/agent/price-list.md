# Price list

Amazon packs, prices and links for the drinks the team wants stocked, checked 11 Nov 2026; copy them into `sourcing`.

The team chose these drinks. Prices are what Amazon showed on 11 Nov 2026 and will change.

**How to use it.** For each product you stock, give one `sourcing` entry: `store: "Amazon"`, `units_per_pack`, the link as `url`, the price as `expected_price_per_pack` and the max as `max_price_per_pack` (price plus about 15%; the worker never pays more).

**Timing.** Amazon orders for a Friday visit should go in by Wednesday 23:59; later, the planner warns.

**What it says:**

- **Energy drinks carry high prices.** They were 45% of our units at $4.00 to $5.00, and the best slot sold at $5.00.
- **20 oz sodas sold at $2.00.** At Amazon's 20 oz prices our floor is $2.70 to $2.90, so we can't match that. A 12 oz can costs about $0.64 a unit, so it can.
- **The history can't tell us how price changes sales.** The 30 Sep repricing had 0 to 15 units on each side while demand was falling. Our own tests have to (`pricing`, step 5).

**Cautions:**

- Sales fell in late October and November because the machine was running empty, not because demand dropped.
- The other team may price differently next quarter. We don't look at its prices (`competition-and-integrity`).
- Replace a reference with our own result once we have two stocked weeks of a product at a price.

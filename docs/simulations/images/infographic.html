What round 1 taught us, and what we changed

Round 1 lessons for the prompt, rules and tools.

Fixed1. It skipped the day-one fill

No receipts looked like "contents unknown", so week 1 sold nothing. The rules card now says the machine starts empty.

Fixed2. Thursday misses Amazon's cutoff

Restocks landed Monday. The prompt now gives each store's cutoff, and the simulator can run on Wednesdays.

Fixed5. Wrong end date and end-stock value

It planned for 31 Jan and treated leftover stock as a loss. The card now says Wed 3 Feb and that leftover stock is valued at cost (still unconfirmed).

Open · course rule3. The price floor priced sodas out

Opus used 2 drinks in 12 slots all season and ended with $900 idle.

Open · needs prices4. Only five drinks are sourced

Four catalog drinks had no sourcing row, so they couldn't be bought.

Open · waiting on David6. One pack per slot

A 20-slot day-one fill of five drinks costs up to about $980, so the fill gets split across visits.

New weak spot: empty slots

The agent refills only after a slot runs out. Seven slots were empty by 13 Jan, and it lost 25 units in weeks 7–8. The rule lost 10.

Next fix to try

Top up a slot when its days of stock left are fewer than the days until the next visit.

Run day: Wednesday, or Thursday plus in-store sourcing. Either way, keep a run on go-live day, Thu 3 Dec.

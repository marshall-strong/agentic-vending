How it is counted

  • Development ends at the ledger's opening entry. A model call before it, live or test, doesn't come out of the $600 (docs/opening-capital-plan.md, #104). The opening is recorded before the day-one fill is fulfilled, about 30 Nov, not on 3 Dec.
  • Every call the harness sends is a row in model_calls; ledger token entries with no such row (older code, or recorded by hand) are added. A call never corrected from its worst case is shown hatched as unconfirmed: it may have cost less, or nothing.
  • Weeks run Saturday to Friday in Eastern time, the ledger's week. Costs are list prices from vending/model_prices.json; the Claude Console's Usage and Cost pages are Anthropic's own record.
  • Live calls after the opening are the competition's money and are not on this page.
